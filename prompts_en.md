# 10 AI Prompts for Your Dream Job

From the vCode video **“10 AI Prompts जो आपको Dream Job दिलाएंगे | CV से Interview तक पूरा Process”**.

**How to use:** click the copy button (top-right of each box), paste into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI chat, and replace every `[paste ...]` with your own text.

> **Honesty rule:** every prompt tells the AI to use only your real experience. Where a number is missing it writes `[ADD METRIC]`: fill in the real number, never a guess. Always read and check the output before you send anything.

## Prompts

1. [Decode the JD](#prompt-1)
2. [Tailor the CV](#prompt-2)
3. [Rewrite the bullets](#prompt-3)
4. [Write the cover letter](#prompt-4)
5. [Build a role-fit matrix](#prompt-5)
6. [Fix ATS alignment](#prompt-6)
7. [Predict interview questions](#prompt-7)
8. [Build STAR answers](#prompt-8)
9. [Get a recruiter-style review](#prompt-9)
10. [Generate a full application pack](#prompt-10)

**Recommended order:** 1 Decode JD → 5 Role-fit matrix → 2 Tailor CV → 3 Bullets → 6 ATS check → 4 Cover letter → 9 Recruiter review (fix and re-run) → 7 Questions → 8 STAR answers → 10 Application pack

<a id="prompt-1"></a>
## Prompt 1: Decode the JD

```text
You are an experienced technical recruiter. Read the job description below and break it
down into one table with four columns: Must-have skills, Responsibilities, ATS keywords
(exact phrases as written in the JD), and Standout traits the hiring manager will look
for. After the table, list the 5 things my CV must prove for this role. Do not assume
anything that is not in the JD.

JD: [paste JD]
```

---

<a id="prompt-2"></a>
## Prompt 2: Tailor the CV

```text
Rewrite my CV for the job below. Change only the summary, the experience bullets and the
skills section, and use the JD's own keywords wherever they truthfully match my
experience. Rules: use only facts from my CV; never invent jobs, skills, tools, degrees
or numbers. If a JD skill is missing from my CV, do not add it — list it at the end
under "Gaps".

CV: [paste CV]
JD: [paste JD]
```

---

<a id="prompt-3"></a>
## Prompt 3: Rewrite the bullets

```text
Rewrite these CV bullets in the format: action verb + what I did + result. Keep each
bullet to one line, align the wording with the JD, and use only the facts given. If a
number or result is missing, write [ADD METRIC] instead of guessing.

Role: [target job title]
JD: [paste JD]
Bullets: [paste bullets]
```

---

<a id="prompt-4"></a>
## Prompt 4: Write the cover letter

```text
Write a cover letter for this role in under 250 words. Pick my 2–3 strongest real
achievements and connect each one to a specific need in the JD. Sound confident and
human, not generic. Use only my real experience; do not invent achievements, numbers or
facts about the company.

Company: [company name]
CV: [paste CV]
JD: [paste JD]
```

---

<a id="prompt-5"></a>
## Prompt 5: Build a role-fit matrix

```text
Compare my background with this JD and build a role-fit matrix with five columns: JD
requirement, My real evidence, Fit (Strong / Partial / Gap), What to highlight in my CV
and cover letter, Interview story to use. Be honest about gaps, and for each gap suggest
a truthful way to frame it (for example a related skill, or what I am learning now). Do
not invent experience.

Background: [paste CV]
JD: [paste JD]
```

---

<a id="prompt-6"></a>
## Prompt 6: Fix ATS alignment

```text
Act as an ATS (applicant tracking system) checker. Compare my CV with this JD and give
me: 1) a match score out of 100 with a one-line reason, 2) important JD keywords missing
from my CV, 3) weak sections, 4) formatting issues that can break ATS parsing, 5) a
rewritten 3-line summary that uses the JD's language. Add a keyword to the summary only
if my CV supports it.

CV: [paste CV]
JD: [paste JD]
```

---

<a id="prompt-7"></a>
## Prompt 7: Predict interview questions

```text
Act as the hiring manager for this role. Write the 15 interview questions you are most
likely to ask me: 6 technical, 5 behavioral and 4 culture fit. Under each question, add
one line on what a strong answer must include, based on my real background.

Background: [paste CV]
JD: [paste JD]
```

---

<a id="prompt-8"></a>
## Prompt 8: Build STAR answers

```text
Using only my real experience, write 8 interview answers in STAR format (Situation,
Task, Action, Result). Cover leadership, problem-solving, conflict, failure and
achievement. Keep each answer under 150 words. Where a result has no number, write [ADD
METRIC]. If my background has no real story for a theme, say so instead of making one
up.

Background: [paste CV]
JD: [paste JD]
```

---

<a id="prompt-9"></a>
## Prompt 9: Get a recruiter-style review

```text
Act as a strict recruiter screening candidates for this role. Review my CV and cover
letter and give one verdict: SHORTLIST, MAYBE or REJECT, with a one-line reason. Then
list 3 strengths, 3 weaknesses and 5 quick fixes I can make in under 30 minutes. Judge
only what is written; do not assume skills I have not shown.

CV: [paste CV]
Cover letter: [paste cover letter]
JD: [paste JD]
```

<a id="prompt-10"></a>
## Prompt 10: Generate a full application pack

```text
Create a complete application pack for this role using only my real experience — no
invented jobs, skills, degrees or numbers:
1) a 3-line CV summary
2) a cover letter under 200 words
3) the 10 interview questions I am most likely to get
4) 5 smart questions I should ask the interviewer
5) a LinkedIn DM to the recruiter, under 80 words
6) a polite follow-up email to send 5 days after applying
Write [ADD METRIC] wherever a number is missing.

Background: [paste CV]
JD: [paste JD]
```
